GO DEEPER

Your Emotional Blueprint.

From childhood, our experiences with parents, caregivers, authority figures, relationships and significant life events can shape how we understand love, safety, worth, trust, rejection, boundaries and connection.

These experiences can influence the protective patterns we develop to cope and feel safe. Over time, these patterns can become so familiar that they feel like “just who I am.”

“A pattern you learned is a pattern you can learn to understand and transform.”
WHAT WE EXPLORE

The Deeper Patterns We Explore

Father Wounds

Early experiences with father figures can influence safety, approval, protection and receiving love.

Mother Wounds

Early experiences around nurturing and acceptance can influence self-worth, self-love and validation.

Rejection & Abandonment

Past rejection or emotional abandonment can heighten sensitivity to withdrawal, exclusion or being left.

Shame

Unresolved shame can quietly influence confidence, self-expression, relationships and the way we see ourselves.

Authority & Power

Experiences with controlling or intimidating authority figures can shape responses to power, criticism and boundaries.

Sexual Trauma

Sexual abuse, coercion, violation or unwanted sexual experiences can affect safety, trust, body awareness, self-worth and intimacy. We approach this work with privacy, sensitivity, dignity and without judgment.

SHADOW AWARENESS

The Shadow Within.

There may be parts of you that you learned to hide — anger, vulnerability, needs, insecurity or the desire to be loved.

These hidden or disowned parts can influence our behaviour without us fully understanding why.

Shadow awareness is not about judging these parts. It is about bringing awareness, understanding and compassion to them.
THE PATTERN

When the Past Meets the Present.

When an old unmet need is activated in a present situation, your reaction can sometimes feel much bigger than the situation itself.

Instead of only asking:
